The 2-Rule Strategy

1

🔍 Break of Structure (BOS)

  • Price must break above a prior swing high (bullish) or below a prior swing low (bearish)
  • Confirmation comes from fractal detection — not just any candle close
  • This is your entry signal trigger
2

📊 FVG + CVD Flow Confirmation

  • A Fair Value Gap must exist at or near the BOS level
  • CVD (Cumulative Delta Volume) must confirm the direction — buy-side or sell-side flow
  • Both conditions must align — BOS + FVG + CVD flow

How It Works

1

OB Fractal FVG identifies order blocks

Detects 2-candle reversal patterns at key price levels, drawing rectangles where institutional orders were placed before major moves.

2

Fractals confirm Break of Structure

Williams Fractals validate that price has broken a genuine swing point — filtering out fake breaks and noise.

3

FVG gaps mark imbalance zones

Fair Value Gaps highlight areas where price action left an unpaid auction — natural magnets for price return.

4

CVD confirms the flow direction

Cumulative Delta Volume shows whether buying or selling pressure is truly driving the move — no flow, no trade.
