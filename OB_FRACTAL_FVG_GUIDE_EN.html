Order Blocks • Fair Value Gaps • Fractals • Break of Structure • Cumulative Delta Volume

Pine Script Indicators for TradingView

Introduction

Two complementary Pine Script indicators form this trading system:

  1. OB Fractal FVG (overlay, chart pane): Detects Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps, Fractals, and Break of Structure directly on the price chart.
  2. CVD by USDT Delta Size (separate pane): A Cumulative Delta Volume indicator with per-bar action classification (BULL, BEAR, ABSb, ABSs), a winsorized table, and absorption arrows.

The CVD provides flow-side confirmation that complements the structural signals from OB Fractal FVG.

OB Fractal FVG — Technical Terms

Order Block (OB) BullishBearish

TermDefinition
2-Candle ReversalBearish engulfing pattern: bar[2] is a down candle, bar[1] is an up candle that closes above bar[2]'s high. For bearish OB: bar[2] is up, bar[1] is down closing below bar[2]'s low.
Body ContainmentThe reaction candle's body fully contains the previous candle's body (prevBodyTop <= reactBodyTop AND prevBodyBottom >= reactBodyBottom).
Undercut ConditionAlternative to body containment: reaction bar's low (low[1]) is below the previous down bar's low (low[2]), indicating a false break before the bounce.
Reaction CandleBar[1] — the candle that reacts to and breaks above/below the previous bar, confirming the OB.
Close Near Top/Bottom 10%The close of the reaction candle is within the top 10% (bullish) or bottom 10% (bearish) of that candle's range.
Fractal ConfirmationA recent fractal high or low (within last 5 bars) must be present near the OB for validity.
MitigationWhen price enters the OB zone: fully mitigated if low <= bottom (bullish) or high >= top (bearish); partially mitigated if price touches but doesn't fully enter.
FreezingWhen fully mitigated, the OB box stops extending right and turns gray, marking it as "used."
Left ExtensionOB boxes extend leftward to include consecutive same-direction candles whose bodies are contained within the reaction candle's body range (up to 20 bars back).
Label Inside OBA "OB" text label is placed at the vertical center of each order block rectangle, repositioned each bar as the box extends.

Fair Value Gap (FVG) BullishBearish

TermDefinition
3-Candle ImbalanceBullish: high[3] < low[1] (gap up between bar[3] and bar[1]). Bearish: low[3] > high[1] (gap down). Bar[2] is the middle candle.
50% Level LineA dotted orange horizontal line drawn at the midpoint of the FVG range, extending right until price touches it.
FVG ExtensionThe FVG box extends right each bar until mitigated (price enters the gap range). When mitigated, it freezes and the 50% line is deleted.
FVG MitigationBullish: low <= FVG bottom (high[3]). Bearish: high >= FVG top (low[3]).

Fractal & Break of Structure (BOS)

TermDefinition
Williams FractalA 5-bar pattern (n=2 left bars, configurable) where the middle bar has the highest high (up fractal) or lowest low (down fractal) among the 5 bars.
Frontier HandlingHandles duplicate extremes on the pattern frontier using strict (>=, <=) and non-strict (<, >) comparisons to avoid false positives.
Recent Fractal WindowFractals within the last 5 bars are considered "recent" and used for confirmation.
Bullish BOSClose breaks above a prior fractal high, with prior close at or below that fractal high.
Bearish BOSClose breaks below a prior fractal low, with prior close at or above that fractal low.
BOS BackgroundGreen (85% transparent) for bullish BOS, red (85% transparent) for bearish BOS.

CVD (Cumulative Delta Volume) — Technical Terms

Core Flow Concepts

TermDefinition
USDT DeltaPer-bar delta = buy_vol - sell_vol, where buy_vol and sell_vol are split by the close's position in the bar range: buy_vol = usd * (close - low) / rng; sell_vol = usd - buy_vol. Bounded to [0, usd] so net delta is always meaningful.
Cumulative CVD TotalRunning sum of delta, added only on confirmed bars (barstate.isconfirmed) to avoid real-time noise. Plotted as a green/red area near zero.
Delta EMA (Momentum)20-period EMA of per-bar delta, plotted as an area chart. Green when positive (buy-side momentum), red when negative (sell-side momentum).
OBV Cross-CheckSynthetic delta must agree with the actual close direction (close > close[1] = OBV up) before classifying as BULL or BEAR. Prevents divergence between volume flow and price action.
Winsorized ScaleThe table columns scale using the 95th percentile of magnitude, so one outlier bar can't wash out the color intensity of the rest.

OB Fractal FVG — Features & Benefits

  • Triple-Confluence Validation: Combines OB, FVG, and Fractal/BOS into a single confluence system — higher signal reliability than any single indicator.
  • Visual Zone Mapping: Colored rectangles with labels (OB, FVG) provide clear visual reference for key supply/demand zones at all times.
  • Dynamic Extension: OB and FVG boxes extend right in real-time until mitigated, keeping the zones "alive" and relevant.
  • Mitigation Feedback: Gray coloring on mitigated zones provides clear visual feedback on which zones have been "used" and which remain valid.
  • 50% FVG Level Lines: Dotted orange mid-line provides a precise entry/reference level within FVG zones.
  • Fractal-Confluence Filter: BOS detection via Williams Fractals adds structural confirmation, reducing false positive OB/FVG signals.
  • Undercut Condition: Bullish OBs also valid when the reaction bar's low undercuts the previous down bar's low — catches fake-breakdowns.
  • OB Labels: "OB" labels positioned at box center and repositioned each bar as boxes extend right.
  • No Repainting: Detection uses confirmed bar logic (barstate.isconfirmed or not barstate.isrealtime).
  • Configurable: Toggle individual components (OB, FVG, BOS, fractals) on/off via input settings.

CVD — Features & Benefits

  • USDT-Based Delta: Delta calculated from USDT volume * close, split by close position in bar range — no reliance on raw coin volume.
  • Action Word Classification: Each bar classified as BULL, BEAR, ABSb, ABSs, or FLAT with clear visual meaning.
  • 2-Bar Confirmation Gate: BULL/BEAR signals require two consecutive confirming bars, reducing false positives. ABSb/ABSs fire immediately to catch hidden flow.
  • OBV Agreement Filter: Synthetic delta must agree with actual close direction before BULL/BEAR classification — prevents flow/price divergence false signals.
  • Winsorized Table Scaling: 95th percentile normalization prevents one outlier bar from washing out the entire table's color intensity.
  • Per-Bar Table: Displays Time, Net Delta, Buy Vol, Sell Vol, and Action word for the last N bars (configurable, default 20).
  • Absorption Arrows: Blue up-triangles (bottom) for ABSb, orange down-triangles (top) for ABSs — visual absorption signals directly on the CVD pane.
  • Wick Quality Gate: Configurable wick threshold (default 60%) rejects bars with excessive wicks that could invalidate the signal.
  • Confirmed-Only Accumulation: CVD total only accumulates on barstate.isconfirmed bars, avoiding real-time noise.
  • CVD Momentum EMA: 20-period EMA of delta plotted as area chart — shows short-term flow momentum direction and strength.

Combined Trading System — How CVD + OB Fractal FVG Work Together

The CVD provides flow-side confirmation for OB-based trades:
  • Bullish OB Entry: When a bullish OB is detected with BOS confirmation, check the CVD pane — a BULL or ABSb action word in the current or recent bar confirms that buy-side flow supports the entry.
  • Bearish OB Entry: When a bearish OB is detected with BOS confirmation, check for a BEAR or ABSs action word in the CVD table to confirm sell-side flow.
  • OB Mitigation Filter: If the CVD shows BEAR or ABSs while price approaches a bullish OB, avoid entry — the flow doesn't support the OB as a demand zone.
  • CVD Divergence Warning: If price makes new highs but CVD delta is declining (negative divergence in the delta EMA), the OB/FVG zone above may fail as supply absorbs demand.
  • 2-Bar Persistence Rule: Use the CVD's 2-bar confirmation gate as an additional filter — wait for two consecutive BULL/BEAR bars before entering an OB-based trade.

Trading Plan — Two Simple Rules for OB Validity

Rule 1: Break of Structure (BOS) Confirmation

The OB must be accompanied by a Break of Structure event. A bullish BOS (close breaks above a recent fractal high) confirms that the market structure has shifted upward, validating the bullish OB as a potential support zone. A bearish BOS (close breaks below a recent fractal low) confirms a downward structure shift, validating the bearish OB as a potential resistance zone.

Rationale: Without BOS confirmation, an OB is just a structural pattern — it lacks the conviction signal that institutional buyers (for bullish OB) or sellers (for bearish OB) are actively participating.

Rule 2: FVG Imbalance Confirmation

A Fair Value Gap imbalance must exist at or near the OB zone. A bullish FVG (gap up between bar[3] high and bar[1] low) at the same price level as a bullish OB strengthens the demand zone. A bearish FVG (gap down between bar[3] low and bar[1] high) at the same level as a bearish OB strengthens the supply zone.

Rationale: The FVG represents an unfilled auction — a price level where market participants failed to find equilibrium. When this aligns with an Order Block (institutional order zone), the confluence creates a high-probability reaction point.

Bonus Rule 3 (CVD Filter): Flow-Confirmed Entry

Check the CVD pane for flow confirmation:

  • For bullish OB entries: require BULL or ABSb action word, ideally 2-bar confirmed.
  • For bearish OB entries: require BEAR or ABSs action word, ideally 2-bar confirmed.
  • If CVD shows opposing flow (BEAR for bullish OB, BULL for bearish OB), avoid the entry.

Exit Strategy

  • OB/FVG Mitigated: When the OB or FVG box turns gray (frozen), the zone has been "used" — consider taking partial profits or exiting.
  • CVD Flow Reversal: If CVD action word changes from BULL to BEAR (or vice versa) with 2-bar confirmation while in a trade, consider reducing or exiting — flow is shifting.
  • Opposite BOS: Exit when price triggers an opposing BOS signal, indicating structure shift against your position.
  • CVD Delta EMA Divergence: Exit if price makes new highs/lows but the CVD delta EMA diverges (fails to confirm).
  • Next OB/FVG: Exit at the next confluence zone (new OB or FVG in the direction of the trade).

Risk Management

  • Never risk more than 1-2% of account equity on a single trade.
  • Always use stop-loss orders — the OB/FVG zone is the stop-loss reference point.
  • If the zone is breached and price closes beyond it with mitigation, exit immediately — the zone failed.
  • If CVD shows FLAT or opposing action at the OB zone, reduce position size or avoid entry entirely.
  • Wait for re-confirmation (BOS + FVG + CVD flow) on a fresh OB before entering a new trade in the same direction.
  • On shorter timeframes, combine multiple OB/FVG confluences; on longer timeframes, a single strong confluence may suffice.
